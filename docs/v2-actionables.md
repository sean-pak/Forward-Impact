# Admin Overview v2: Actionables

Context for redesigning the TPW Admin Overview so it supports **conversations that pitch the governor on program efficacy and reach**.

- **Source design (v1):** Figma `Pingo` → frame `TPW Admin · Overview` (node `769:20`)
  https://www.figma.com/design/XZMMUHNldLFqkHrfZuhQqx/Pingo?node-id=769-20
- **Critique date:** 2026-10-25
- **Primary user of this page:** the TPW executive team, preparing for and presenting in conversations with the governor's office and other state officials.
- **Job of the page:** answer "How many people did we help, where, and does it work?" in about 30 seconds, using numbers that hold up under scrutiny.

## Summary

v1 is careful and honest: it labels simulated data, shows n/N with every rate, suppresses small cells, and states its caveats. Keep all of that.

The problem is structure. v1 is an **operations console**: the most valuable space goes to the outreach funnel and staff queue, while reach, outcomes, and cost avoided sit lower on the page. v2 should be a **briefing page** that tells one story in order, and the ops content should move to the pages where staff act on it.

## Guardrails (from PRD/CLAUDE.md, non-negotiable)

Every v2 change must keep these:

- A rate always shows its numerator and denominator (n/N).
- Suppressed cells say `suppressed`, never 0. Suppression rules: denominator < 30, counts of 1–10, facilities with < 3 reporting orgs.
- Simulated outcomes are labeled **SIMULATED** wherever they appear.
- Only CBO-confirmed data is counted.
- No org-vs-org rankings.
- Any generated sentence (e.g. talking points) comes from **code templates over computed numbers**, not LLM prose.
- No identifiers anywhere on the page.

## P0: Story and hierarchy

### 1. Reorder the page around the pitch narrative

**Problem:** v1 order is KPIs → Reporting response + Needs attention → Outcomes → By prison / By program → Estimated impact. The pitch content sits below internal workflow, and $12.4M is the last card.

**v2 order:**
1. Headline / talking points (see #2)
2. Reach KPIs
3. Outcomes vs. state benchmark
4. Estimated impact (cost)
5. Where we work (map + by prison)
6. What works (by program type, with outcomes)
7. Data notes / methodology footer

**Done when:** a first-time viewer can state reach, the outcome comparison, and the cost figure without scrolling past the second screen.

### 2. Add a "Talking points" block at the top

**Problem:** the page shows numbers but gives the presenter nothing ready to say.

**Do:**
- 3–4 sentences, each built from a code template over confirmed metrics, each with its caveat inline. Examples:
  - "TPW member programs reached **2,847 people** in **24 of California's 31 prisons** this fall."
  - "**1,612 participants completed** a program (71.4% of 2,258 enrolled)."
  - "Among participants released at least 3 years ago, **17.0% returned** to prison, compared with 20.4% of all CDCR releases (FY 2020-21). *Association, not cause. SIMULATED.*"
- A copy button on each sentence.
- The same sentences appear in the "Download one-pager" output.

**Done when:** every sentence is generated by a template, matches the numbers on the page, and carries the SIMULATED label where needed.

### 3. Move ops content off the exec overview

**Problem:** the "Reporting response" funnel (`769:211`) and "Needs attention" queue (`769:257`) are staff tasks ("Awaiting confirmation 9", "Nudge scheduled Thursday"). They are well designed but don't belong in a pitch.

**Do:** move both to **Outreach** / **Submissions**. On the overview, keep one compact data-freshness line instead, e.g. "Fall 2026 cycle · 38 orgs confirmed · updated 2 min ago".

**Alternative:** add an "Exec briefing / Ops" view toggle. Briefing is the default for the Executive team account.

## P0: KPI row

### 4. Rebuild the KPI row around reach

**Problem:** the first KPI is "Orgs reporting **38 of 120** · 32% response · goal 60%", which tells a governor that most partners didn't report. Participants (the headline number) gets the same weight as everything else. Hours, a strong volume metric, isn't shown.

**v2 KPIs:**

| Slot | Metric | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hero (larger) | **Participants: 2,847** | Subline: "+412 since spring cycle" |
| 2 | **Completions: 1,612** | Subline: "71.4% · 1,612 / 2,258" |
| 3 | **Programming hours** | Sum of confirmed hours; currently only in the table |
| 4 | **Prisons reached: 24 of 31** | Subline: "**7 with no programs**" (promote "program deserts") |

- Move "Orgs reporting / response rate" to Outreach.
- Remove the "stated rule" label next to completion rate, or explain it in a tooltip.

## P0: Outcomes card

### 5. Make the benchmark comparison the main visual

**Problem:** the most persuasive comparison, program 17.0% vs. CDCR 20.4%, is in a small beige side panel (`770:152`).

**Do:**
- Make the main visual two bars side by side (program vs. state) for the 3-year window. Show the difference in plain language: "3.4 points lower" (and relative, "about 1 in 6 fewer returns", only if computed in code).
- Keep the 1/2/3-year figures as secondary small figures.
- Keep the "Association, not cause" caveat attached to the comparison, not in fine print.

### 6. Fix the misleading rate bars

**Problem:** the 1/2/3-year bars (`770:132`, `770:141`, `770:150`) are green progress bars, so a fuller bar reads as "better" when it means *more people returned to prison*. Their scale is also unclear: 17% fills about 70% of the track.

**Do:** remove them, or redraw them in a neutral color on a labeled 0–100% (or shared, labeled) scale. Use green only for "good direction" metrics.

### 7. Show uncertainty and cohort mismatch

**Problem:** the 3-year n is 171, and the benchmark compares participants with *all* CDCR releases from a different year. Expect pushback from state staff.

**Do:**
- Show a confidence range or a plain "small sample (n=171)" flag next to each rate.
- Put the cohort difference in the benchmark label itself: "CDCR, all releases, FY 2020-21 (different cohort)".

## P0: Estimated impact

### 8. Lead with a defensible estimate, not only the upper bound

**Problem:** "$12.4M cost avoided" is labeled UPPER BOUND, and it's the only number shown. In a meeting it will be quoted as fact or dismissed.

**Do:**
- Show a range (conservative → upper bound), e.g. "$X–12.4M". Put the conservative figure first. The assumptions come from the assumptions table and are computed in code.
- Move this card up to position 4 (see #1).
- Keep the note on assumptions, but tighten it to one line plus a "How we estimate this" link.

## P1: Efficacy and context

### 9. Answer "what works", not just "how many"

**Problem:** "By program type" (`770:249`) shows participant counts only.

**Do:** add completion rate (n/N) per type, and return rate per type when available, subject to suppression. Sort by participants; don't rank orgs.

### 10. Add trend over time

**Problem:** the only sign of change over time is "+412 since spring cycle".

**Do:** add a small cycle-over-cycle chart (participants and completions per cycle). Growth is a core pitch argument.

### 11. Add a California map

**Problem:** geography only appears as a table showing 7 of 24 facilities.

**Do:** add a map of the 31 prisons. Covered prisons are shaded by participants; the 7 without programs are marked clearly. This supports both reach ("24 of 31") and need ("7 with no programs"), and connects to legislators' districts. Keep the table below it, or behind "View all prisons".

### 12. Make room for one human proof point (optional)

A slot for one consented, de-identified quote or story from a member org. It must be manually approved, must not be LLM-generated, and must contain no identifiers.

## P2: Polish and hygiene

### 13. Remove dead space
- Reporting response card: 88px empty spacer (`772:173`).
- By program type card: 125px empty spacer (`770:291`).
Both cards are stretched to match their neighbors. Size cards to their content, or fill them with content from #9 and #10.

### 14. Collapse suppressed rows
Pelican Bay and Ironwood (`770:228`, `770:237`) take full row height with five "suppressed" cells each. Replace them with one line: "2 facilities suppressed (fewer than 3 reporting orgs)".

### 15. Make credibility text readable
The n/N lines, custody-check footer, and assumption notes use small, low-contrast gray monospace text. This is the text that makes the numbers trustworthy, so it needs to be readable. Check against WCAG AA (4.5:1). Consider the sans font at 13px instead of mono for longer lines.

### 16. Remove jargon from the exec view
Rewrite or hide on the exec view: "stated rule", "mock locator 100%", "RAC + EMC + MCC", "custody lookups", "Clarification rounds", "Reply channel". Use a tooltip or the methodology footer for definitions.

### 17. Demote "Run custody lookups"
It's a sensitive operational action and appears as a prominent button on the Executive team's overview. Move it to Outcomes (or behind a confirm step on an ops page). Keep "Download one-pager" as the primary action.

### 18. Add useful filters
"All prisons" is the only filter. Add **cycle / date range** and **program type**. These matter more for pitch prep.

## Keep from v1

- n/N on every rate, "suppressed" not 0, SIMULATED labels everywhere, the "association, not cause" note.
- Serif numerals, restrained green and beige palette, card rhythm. They read as credible, not salesy.
- Identity vault status in the sidebar.
- The funnel and attention queue components (reuse them on Outreach / Submissions).
- "Download one-pager". v2 should make the on-screen order match the one-pager.
